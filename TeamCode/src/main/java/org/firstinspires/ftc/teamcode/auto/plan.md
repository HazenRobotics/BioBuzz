## Ideal Auto Scenario (EX. Red alliance 48 point auto)
* Start Autonomous
* Launcher powers up and accurately shoots three preloaded pollen into the cell
* cell tips and we gain 20 points
* drive to flower adjacent to the starting position of the robot and release and intake 3 balls (now 4 balls stored in robot) (moving in auto gives 3 points)
* drive to shooting position on the opposite side of the field
* line up and shoot 4 pollen into the cell
* drive to flower that is right next to the robot and release and intake all 4 balls (now 4 ball stored in robot)
* go back to shooting position
* line up and shoot 4 pollen into cell
* cell tips and we gain 20 points
* drive to parking zone (parking gives 5 points)
